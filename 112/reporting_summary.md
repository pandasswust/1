# Reporting summary

This file records the study-design, statistics, and reproducibility details that
are usually requested by Nature-family journals.

## Study type

Algorithm development with secondary public-data benchmarking and controlled
ROS/Gazebo co-simulation. No human participants, animals, or clinical data were
involved.

## Data provenance

- NYU Depth V2: public secondary data, verified 51-frame balanced subset
  (17 Flat, 17 Stairs, 17 Obstacle), with all 17 frames containing at least 100
  annotated stair pixels retained.
- Go1 street: primary simulated data generated by the released Gazebo world,
  launch files, and perception/SAPF nodes.
- Standalone SAPF: synthetic but released kinematic profile with paired seeds.
- Cross-illumination: a photometrically shifted version of the same simulated
  geometry.

## Sample sizes

- NYU clustering: 51 frames; 20 seeds per degradation level.
- Go1 calibration: 351 frames (59/152/85/55); 5 temporally blocked folds.
- Go1 closed loop: 3 nominal + 3 degraded 30 s runs.
- Standalone SAPF: 20 paired seeds, 1000 particles.
- Label-efficiency curve: 25 blocked evaluations per calibration fraction.
- Runtime: 5 repeated timing blocks per operation; single-thread CPU.

## Randomization and blinding

Seed-controlled random initialization, degradation assignment, and subset
selection. Comparisons are paired across identical seeds/runs. Blinding is not
applicable to algorithmic benchmarks; ground-truth labels were fixed before
model fitting and were not used to tune the online weights.

## Statistical methods

- Paired two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank tests over 20 seeds, Ours vs each
  baseline, clean and severe.
- Exact p-values reported; no family-wise correction because the comparisons
  are exploratory. The severe depth-only tie (p = 0.797) is reported rather
  than hidden.
- Dispersion: standard deviation for 20-seed accuracy; s.e.m. for the
  label-efficiency curve across 25 blocked evaluations.
- Effect sizes: mean paired gain in percentage points and win/loss/tie counts.

## Software and hardware

- Python 3.8; scikit-learn 1.3.2; SciPy 1.10.1; NumPy; Matplotlib; h5py.
- ROS 1 and Gazebo for the closed loop.
- CPU-only measurements; no GPU inference is used by the proposed online path.
- All figures are vector PDFs plus 1000 dpi PNGs generated by released scripts.

## Inclusion and exclusion

No samples were excluded post hoc. The NYU subset is limited by the public
annotation: only 17 frames satisfy the stair criterion. This is declared as a
pilot-scale limitation. The raw 2.97 GB archive is not redistributed; the
extraction script reproduces the subset.

## Reproducibility

- Every plotted value has a tidy CSV in `source_data/`.
- Every data file, model, prediction, and trajectory used in the paper is
  listed in `upload_manifest.md`.
- `checksums.sha256` covers the deliverables.
- `scripts/verify_paper_numbers.py` re-checks the manuscript numbers,
  propositions, and figure files.

## Limitations

Pilot-scale NYU subset; one simulated street; repeatable kinematic trajectory
rather than a physical quadruped; obstacle class is a chair proxy. Physical
robot trials and large-scale outdoor benchmarks are future work.
